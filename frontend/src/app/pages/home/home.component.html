Hello! My name is Ryan Burgess and this is Comics Tracker!

Below you will find some comic shops I recommend checking out in the Greensboro, NC area.

These shops had a profound impact on me as I first got into comic books and they'll ultimately be a better resource than my app ever could be. Hope you check them out!

Acme Comics

Greensboro's Original Since 1983

Address: 2150 Lawndale Dr, Greensboro, NC 27408

Phone: (336) 574-2263

From Acme Comics: "New and used comic books, graphic novels, figurines and toys are sold at this store, which is known for its friendly service. Acme Comics is a locally owned and operated, full service comic book store. Established in 1983, we are the oldest and largest comic book store in the Greensboro, High Point, and Winston Salem North Carolina region collectively known as The Triad."

Strange Ways Comics & More

(Formerly Acme Comics Downtown)

Address: 335 S Davie St, Greensboro, NC 27401

Phone: (743) 209-0940

From Strange Ways: "Strange Ways Comics and More isn't just a comic book store, toy store, or video store. Strange Ways is a gathering space for those who have felt left out of conventional third space. We intend specialize in a diverse range of comics, graphic novels, collectibles, and vintage VHS tapes, ensuring there's something for everyone... Join us when we open our doors in January 2026 and be part of the excitement!"

Parts Unknown the Comic Book Store

Since August 9th, 1989

Address: 906 Spring Garden St, Greensboro, NC 27403

Phone: (336) 272-7060

From Parts Unknown: "Comic book shop (including graphic novels), with graphic novel sections and a friendly staff. Parts Unknown opened its doors on August 9th 1989 at the now vacant Cotton Mill Square Outlet Mall in Greensboro, NC. And it is still open today at 906 Spring Garden Street. I have been a fan of comic books since the fourth grade and I used to dream about a store that sold comics. I even used to walk around Greensboro hoping to find one. That is why I decided to open my own store.""
